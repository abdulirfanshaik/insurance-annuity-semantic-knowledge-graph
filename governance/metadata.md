# Metadata & Governance

For each governed concept capture:
- business name and definition;
- steward/owner role;
- authoritative source;
- physical mappings;
- datatype/cardinality;
- quality rules;
- lineage;
- sensitivity classification;
- version/effective date;
- related ontology class/property.

Example: **Beneficiary** maps to active `contract_party_role` records where `role_type='BENEFICIARY'` and may carry allocation percentage and effective dates.
