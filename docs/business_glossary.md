# Business Glossary

| Term | Definition | Example Source |
|---|---|---|
| Annuity Contract | Contract instance associated with an annuity product and contractual parties | CONTRACT |
| Contract Owner | Party holding the OWNER role for a contract during an effective period | CONTRACT_PARTY_ROLE |
| Annuitant | Party holding the ANNUITANT role | CONTRACT_PARTY_ROLE |
| Beneficiary | Party designated with BENEFICIARY role and, where applicable, allocation percentage | CONTRACT_PARTY_ROLE |
| Contribution | Inflow transaction classified as CONTRIBUTION | CONTRACT_TRANSACTION |
| Distribution | Outflow event classified as DISTRIBUTION | CONTRACT_TRANSACTION |
| Account Value | Contract value captured at a defined as-of date | ACCOUNT_VALUE |
| Producer of Record | Producer relationship classified as PRIMARY for the effective period | CONTRACT_PRODUCER |
| Active Contract | Contract whose governed status is ACTIVE | CONTRACT |
