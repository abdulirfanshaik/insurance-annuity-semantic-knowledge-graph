# Insurance & Annuity Domain

## Core Concepts
**Party/Customer** — person or organization participating in the business relationship.

**Policy** — insurance agreement with product, status, effective dates and insured/owner relationships.

**Annuity Contract** — contract designed to accumulate value and/or provide distributions according to product and contract terms.

**Owner** — party with ownership rights over a contract.

**Annuitant** — person whose life/age is associated with annuity benefits or payout calculations.

**Beneficiary** — party designated to receive applicable benefits according to contractual rules.

**Producer / Agency** — licensed distribution participant associated with sale or servicing of a product/contract.

**Product** — governed product definition. Annuity subtypes may include fixed, indexed and variable classifications in the synthetic model.

**Rider** — optional or contractual feature associated with a product/contract.

**Contract Transaction** — contribution/premium, withdrawal, distribution, surrender or other financial event.

**Account Value** — modeled contract value at an as-of date.

**Underwriting Case** — assessment process associated with applicable insurance business processes.

**Claim** — request for benefits under a policy/contract where applicable.

## Lifecycle Example
Application -> Review -> Issued -> Active -> Distribution/Servicing -> Matured/Surrendered/Terminated.
